Device Selection

Use this panel to choose how many devices you want to connect, up to a maximum of eight.

Serial Port and Baud Rate

Each device has a serial port and baud rate to choose.

The list of serial ports refreshes every five seconds.

If a selected serial port disconnects, the application remembers it and selects it again when the port reconnects.

The baud rate must match the device. 3D printers commonly use 115200 baud, while many Arduino sketches use 9600 baud.
