Serial Communication

Once the devices are selected, click Start to connect to them. Click Stop when you want to change the device setup or close the connections.

Connection Status

  • Disconnected: communication has not started, or the port is no longer available.
  • Connected: the port is open and ready to use.
  • Not working: the application tried to open the port but could not.

If something goes wrong, hover over the status badge for details. The same error will also appear in the terminal.

While communication is running, the device selectors are locked to prevent accidental changes.
